Methods and data

Seven Home Credit tables are joined at applicant level. The model uses payment timing, earlier decisions, monthly balances, and application amounts. Changing a monetary input also recomputes the related ratios. Remaining features come from training-group medians. Missing values follow the tree’s learned rules.

This edition trains on 246,008 applications and evaluates on 61,503 untouched applications. Three-fold cross-validation within the training partition selects the leaf count. The scores describe the benchmark model on historical data. The form uses fictional profiles and runs entirely on your device.
